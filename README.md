## What is AIExportHub?

AIExportHub is a collection of tools for exporting, backing up, and organizing conversations created across multiple AI platforms.

A growing amount of useful work now lives inside AI chats:

- research,
- code,
- writing,
- product decisions,
- study notes,
- business analysis,
- images and files,
- project knowledge,
- ideas worth revisiting later.

The problem is that this knowledge is scattered across separate AI services.

AIExportHub helps move it into formats you control.

> **Own your AI history. Keep it portable. Turn it into knowledge.**

# Why AIExportHub?

AI platforms are great places to create knowledge.

They are not always the best places to keep it forever.

Conversations can become difficult to find, platforms change, export formats evolve, and useful work ends up split across multiple products.

AIExportHub is built around a few simple ideas:

### Your AI conversations should belong to you

Important conversations should not remain locked inside a single platform.

### Portable formats matter

Notion, Markdown, Obsidian, and PDF make your data easier to search, organize, back up, and reuse.

### Exporting is only the first step

Saving a conversation is useful.

Being able to find and use it again months later is much more valuable.

```text

      ↓
 AIExportHub
      ↓

      ↓
````

Or, more simply:

```text

Organize
   ↓

   ↓
```

Connect conversations to projects, categories, and context.

### Retrieve

Find previous work when you need it again.

### Rediscover

Bring back useful knowledge that you forgot existed.

## 🐛 Something is broken

Please open a bug report:

**[https://github.com/joysey/ai-exporter-hub/issues/new/choose](https://github.com/joysey/ai-exporter-hub/issues/new/choose)**

When reporting a bug, please include:

* which AI platform you are using,
* export destination,
* extension / plugin version,
* browser,
* operating system,
* what you were trying to do,
* what happened,
* what you expected to happen,
* whether the issue affects one conversation or many,
* screenshots or error messages if available.

### Important

Please remove private or sensitive conversation content before uploading screenshots, logs, or sample files.

---

## 🔄 Export or compatibility problem

AI platforms change frequently.

If a conversation suddenly fails to export, formatting changes, images disappear, conversation detection stops working, or a specific content type cannot be handled correctly, please report it.

Useful details include:

* AI platform,
* conversation type,
* single or batch export,
* approximate conversation size,
* whether images or files are included,
* exact error message,
* whether the same problem occurs in another conversation.

# Product philosophy

## Local and portable when possible

Your knowledge should remain useful even if the original AI platform changes.

## Preserve context

A useful export is more than copied text.

Where supported, AIExportHub aims to preserve things such as:

* message structure,
* code,
* tables,
* links,
* citations,
* images,
* attachments,
* project information,
* source URLs,
* formatting.

## Avoid lock-in

The goal is not to move your AI history from one locked platform into another locked platform.

The goal is to give you more control over where your knowledge lives.

## Knowledge over storage

Backing up conversations matters.

But the more interesting question is:

> **How can we use what we have already learned?**
